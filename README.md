# Giggle
### Discover Skills. Exchange Value. Create Opportunities.

Giggle is an accessible digital services marketplace and interactive collaboration platform designed to connect people through digital services, skills, and creativity.

Unlike conventional freelance marketplaces, Giggle allows users to buy, sell, exchange, and offer services for free through a single unified account.

The goal is to make digital opportunities accessible to everyone, regardless of their financial position or professional experience.

---

## 1. Project Vision

Giggle combines three major experiences:

- **Marketplace:** Discover, purchase, sell, and offer digital services.
- **Collaboration:** Exchange skills, connect with creators, and collaborate.
- **Community:** Network, share knowledge, and participate in creative discussions.

Giggle is inspired by the convenience of platforms such as Fiverr, but aims to establish its own identity through dual-role accounts, skill exchange, free services, and integrated collaboration.

## 2. Core Product Principles

1. One account for both buyers and sellers.
2. Guest browsing without mandatory registration.
3. Accessible opportunities for beginners and experienced creators.
4. Support for paid, free, and skill-exchange services.
5. Simple and intuitive user experiences.
6. Secure transactions and trustworthy interactions.
7. An integrated AI assistant.
8. Responsive experiences across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## 3. Account System

Giggle will support:

- Guest visitors.
- Registered standard users.
- Premium subscribers.
- Platform administrators.

Every registered standard or premium user can simultaneously purchase and offer services without switching accounts.

### Premium Membership

Proposed subscription: $20/month.

Planned benefits include:

- Increased upload capacity.
- Higher file limits.
- Additional trial allowances.
- Live messaging and negotiation tools.
- Enhanced gig visibility.
- Priority inbound orders and gig requests.
- Ad-free browsing.

## 4. Marketplace

Users can discover and interact with three types of services:

### Paid Services
Creators offer services at specified prices.

### Free Services
Creators offer services without monetary payment, helping beginners build experience and portfolios.

### Skill Exchange
Users exchange services and skills instead of paying money.

Marketplace functionality will eventually include search, categories, filters, service packages, creator profiles, ratings, reviews, and order management.

## 5. Orders and Transactions

The planned order lifecycle includes:

1. Buyer selects a service.
2. Buyer submits an order request.
3. Seller receives and reviews the request.
4. Seller accepts or declines.
5. Accepted orders proceed to fulfillment.
6. Seller delivers the work.
7. Buyer reviews and confirms completion.
8. Transactions are finalized.

Future functionality includes revisions, cancellations, refunds, disputes, transaction protection, and seller payouts.

The proposed platform commission is 10% of completed monetary transactions.

## 6. Communication and Community

Giggle will support two distinct communication experiences:

- Transactional messaging for buyers and sellers.
- Community discussions for networking, learning, and collaboration.

Future features include direct messaging, notifications, community channels, and collaboration tools.

## 7. AI Assistant

Giggle will include an integrated AI assistant to help users:

- Understand platform features.
- Navigate the application.
- Create and improve service listings.
- Resolve common platform-related problems.
- Receive guidance when using Giggle.

## 8. Administration

A dedicated administrative environment will eventually provide:

- User and account management.
- Marketplace moderation.
- Platform announcements.
- Activity monitoring.
- Traffic and analytics dashboards.
- Transaction oversight.
- Platform configuration and operational controls.

## 9. Brand Identity

Giggle's visual identity should be:

- Modern.
- Professional.
- Welcoming.
- Accessible.
- Distinctive.
- Technology-oriented.

### Color Direction

Primary visual palette:

- White.
- Violet and purple.
- Light sky blue.
- Green as a recognizable brand accent.

The interface should use consistent typography, spacing, icons, components, and visual hierarchy.

Avoid generic marketplace templates and excessive decorative effects.

## 11. Current Development Objective

The immediate objective is to develop a serious, functional frontend prototype suitable for:

- Product demonstrations.
- Investor presentations.
- Early product evaluation.
- Testing user journeys.
- Gathering feedback.

This is not yet the complete production platform.

The prototype should use realistic mock data and simulated services where necessary.

Core experiences should be interactive rather than static visual mockups.

Real payment processing, advanced authentication, production security, and other infrastructure-heavy features can be implemented in later development phases.

## 12. Development Guidelines

All contributors and AI coding agents should follow these principles:

1. Inspect the existing project before making changes.
2. Preserve established architecture and working functionality.
3. Prefer reusable components over duplicated code.
4. Keep business logic separate from presentation.
5. Keep mock data separate from UI components.
6. Centralize temporary service implementations.
7. Prepare clear API integration boundaries.
8. Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
9. Maintain responsive layouts.
10. Test important interactions after implementation.
11. Avoid large, uncontrolled modifications.
12. Document significant architectural decisions.

Do not rebuild the entire application when implementing an individual feature.

Work incrementally and preserve a stable, testable codebase.

## 13. Long-Term Vision

Giggle aims to become an inclusive digital ecosystem where people can discover opportunities, monetize their abilities, exchange knowledge, and collaborate regardless of their financial circumstances.

The long-term ambition extends beyond a conventional freelance marketplace.
